# Gridwell

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite, confirmed by the user. Browser-local synthetic simulation; no backend or account required.

## Users

A Colorado distribution system operator coordinating inspections and conditional repairs following a wildfire-related public safety power shutoff. Hackathon judges must also understand the demonstration without energy expertise.

## Product Purpose

Help a human review the next eligible inspection or repair when new field information changes the work queue. Preserve the team's wording: “What if we can efficiently and intelligently prioritize powerline repair for high risk areas/customers? (Reactive)”

## Operating Context

Colorado Impact Hackathon, October 21, 2026. The 2026 challenge is clean, secure, and resilient electric grids; this prototype focuses on resilience. The 2025 worksheet is a process reference, not current competition rules.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Presentation-only dashboard, not a whole product (user clarification). Synthetic network, two crews plus one available through a demo scenario, field updates, hard safety and upstream dependency gates, explainable ranking, operator review, and scenario comparison. Demonstrate a hospital losing backup power, unsafe access, and an additional crew. No actual switching, grid connections, personal medical records, live hazards, or utility topology. Planned shutoffs do not imply damaged lines. Work completion is inspection/repair clearance in the model, never permission to energize. Model outputs are illustrative and cannot establish real-world savings or legal compliance.

## Brand Commitments

The user requests Google Sans for every text element, white surfaces with Google blue/red/yellow/green accents, square corners without rounded cards, and a real geographic map with clear feeder lines. Use the supplied map reference as visual inspiration, not factual utility data. Preserve the original desktop structure: left navigation, summary, map, candidate tasks, safety notice and legend. Gridwell is an implementation name, not an existing organization or an endorsement.

## Evidence on Hand
